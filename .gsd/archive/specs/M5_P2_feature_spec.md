## Phase Summary

Milestone 5's user-visible outcome is: *"Log timestamped gravity/temperature readings through fermentation, watch a gravity-and-temp chart and live attenuation build up, then carry the batch through Conditioning to Completed with measured FG, ABV, carbonation, tasting notes and a 1–5 rating — one real brew, planned and logged end to end."*

**M5_P1 delivered the roadmap's first hardening bullet** (the `Reading` entity, the chart, live attenuation, and `Brewing → Fermenting`) and is verification-clean at 50/50 criteria. **M5_P2 — this phase — delivers the second bullet and closes the milestone**: `Fermenting → Conditioning → Completed`, measured OG/FG/ABV/attenuation/efficiency displayed against the frozen estimates, carbonation (build-spec §3.7) promoted into `packages/calculations` under test, timestamped `BatchNote` entries, tasting notes and a 1–5 rating — and then **the MVP acceptance run**, which is `.gsd/ROADMAP.md`'s stated verification threshold for Milestone 5 and, per `.gsd/STATE.json`'s `notes.mvp_completes_at`, **the boundary at which the MVP the user approved at the roadmap gate is complete**.

**User-visible outcome of this phase:** take a real batch from Fermenting through bottling into Conditioning and finally close it as Completed — recording measured final gravity, a carbonation method and target with the priming-sugar or force-carbonation figure computed for you, tasting notes and a 1–5 rating — see every measured figure side by side with the estimate the recipe predicted, keep a timestamped note log that records which stage each note was written at, and have the whole history still read back intact after a full app restart.

**Two long-running carryovers are closed here, not passed on again.** (1) The M4_P1 **measured-efficiency live-recompute** observation (`.gsd/archive/CRITIC_REPORT_M4_P1_AC12.md`, deferred once by M5_P1's deviation 7) is resolved by freezing the measured comparison figures at `Completed`, on the same discipline `recipeSnapshot` and `statsSnapshot` already use — and by deleting the duplicated derivation from `BatchDetail.tsx` in favour of one shared pure function. (2) The **`DryHop` `timeMinutes` retirement** is deferred once more — but with fresh reasoning, a stated definition of done, and an explicit placement decision put to the user at the halt gate rather than a third silent inheritance (§4 deviation 9).

### Key Behaviors

1. **The pipeline completes, and it still has exactly one definition.** `BatchStatus` gains `'Conditioning'` and `'Completed'`; `BATCH_STATUSES` and `BATCH_STATUS_TRANSITIONS` widen **together** in the same edit, and the AJV route enum and the UI `<select>` follow by derivation — the property M5_P1's AC-41(c) exists to prove. `Completed` is **terminal**: no transition leaves it, in either direction. No stage may be skipped or reversed.
2. **Closing a batch freezes its measured figures, on the same discipline as `statsSnapshot`.** The transition into `Completed` computes a `ClosingSnapshot` **once**, server-side, and stores it in a new nullable `closing_snapshot` column. Measured ABV, apparent attenuation, mash efficiency, priming sugar and force-carbonation PSI are read from that snapshot forever after. A later change to the ABV formula (ROADMAP.md Milestone 7 swaps it explicitly) or to the carbonation polynomials (Milestone 8's calculators use the same functions) **cannot move a closed batch's numbers**. This is the M4_P1 carryover's resolution, in the phase M5_P1 named for it.
3. **A frozen figure and a recomputed figure never occupy the same unlabelled slot.** Where a value can only be recomputed, it is recomputed **and labelled** — the `(recalculated)` discipline M4_P1's AC-12(e) established, extended to the measured side. Where a value cannot honestly be produced at all, it is **suppressed** — no `0`, no `—`, no `NaN`, no fabricated default. Priming sugar with no temperature reading is suppressed rather than defaulted, because an over-estimated priming figure is a physical safety hazard, not a cosmetic one.
4. **A note records the stage it was written at, and the server decides both.** `BatchNote.timestamp` and `BatchNote.status` are server-minted at creation from the server clock and the batch's stored status; neither is ever accepted from a client, and neither is ever rewritten by an edit. This is the reason M5_P1 deferred `BatchNote` to this phase: only from here on do all five statuses exist, so the field's domain is complete on the day it ships.
5. **Nothing already frozen moves.** `recipe_snapshot` and `stats_snapshot` are untouched by every write path this phase adds — including the completion write, every note write and every carbonation edit. M4_P1's AC-12 property and M5_P1's AC-32 property both still hold and are re-asserted here.

### Resolved Ambiguities (Binding)

Every item below is binding on execution and on the critic audit. Where a bound is stated, the **operator** is stated.

- **The five statuses and their transitions, exhaustively.** `BATCH_STATUSES` is exactly `['Planning','Brewing','Fermenting','Conditioning','Completed']` in that order. `BATCH_STATUS_TRANSITIONS` is exactly `{ Planning: ['Planning','Brewing'], Brewing: ['Brewing','Fermenting'], Fermenting: ['Fermenting','Conditioning'], Conditioning: ['Conditioning','Completed'], Completed: ['Completed'] }`. Every entry includes its own key (re-saving without a status change is always permitted). **`Completed` is terminal** — `Completed → Conditioning` is rejected exactly as `Fermenting → Brewing` is. Of the **25** ordered pairs, exactly **9** are legal.
- **The completion gate, with its exact operator.** A write whose resulting status is `'Completed'` — i.e. **every** write where `data.status === 'Completed'`, **regardless of the batch's current stored status**, including a `Completed → Completed` re-save — requires **both** `measuredOg !== null` **and** `measuredFg !== null` — evaluated against the **effective post-write values** (i.e. the values in the PUT body, since a batch `PUT` is a full replace, never a merge). If either is `null`, the response is `400 VALIDATION_FAILED` with a message naming **each** missing field, **nothing is written** (status unchanged, no snapshot created, `updated_at` unchanged). The check is `!== null`, never a falsy check. *Why both:* the roadmap's own verification threshold is *"calculated attenuation and ABV from the measured OG/FG match a hand-calculation"* — both are inputs to that, and requiring them makes the frozen snapshot total instead of a lattice of nullable arms. **BINDING — corrected by the 2026-08-09 third amendment.** An earlier draft of this bullet (and of §2.7 step 5) carried an additional conjunct, `existing.status !== 'Completed'`, which would have **disabled the gate on every re-save of an already-`Completed` batch**. That reading is wrong and must not be implemented: a `Completed → Completed` re-save with `measuredFg` nulled would pass the gate, then satisfy write-condition **W2** (a closing-snapshot input changed by strict `!==`), and rebuild `closing_snapshot` with `NaN` in `abv` and `apparentAttenuationPct` — silently corrupting a frozen record, which is exactly what Key Behavior 2 exists to prevent. The gate is unconditional on `data.status === 'Completed'`; that is what **AC-21** has always required in its literal text, and it is what the shipped implementation already does. This is a **prose-only correction with zero code change** — the implementation was already correct and AC-21 already traces YES against it.
- **The closing snapshot is written on exactly two conditions, and omitted otherwise.** `batchRepository.update()` receives a **distinct explicit parameter**, `closingSnapshotOverride`, never merged in from `BatchWriteInput` (which does not carry the field). The route computes a value for it iff **(W1)** the write's resulting status is `'Completed'` **and** the stored `closingSnapshot` is `null`; **or (W2)** the stored status is already `'Completed'` **and** at least one closing-snapshot input differs from its stored value by strict `!==` — the inputs being exactly `measuredOg`, `measuredFg`, `measuredPreBoilGravity`, `measuredBottlingSizeL`, `carbonationType`, `carbonationVolumesTarget`, `carbonationTempC`. In every other case the column is **omitted from `.set()` entirely** and is therefore byte-identical afterward.
- **Reading writes never touch the closing snapshot — deliberate asymmetry, stated rather than left to be found.** `POST`/`PUT`/`DELETE` on `/api/batches/:batchId/readings/*` never recompute `closing_snapshot`, even though `peakFermentationTempC` is derived from readings. *Reasoning:* a correction to `measuredFg` is a correction to **the record of the close itself**, so re-freezing is right; a reading logged after the close is by definition **after** the beer was packaged, and cannot retroactively change the priming sugar that was actually used. The same applies to note writes.
- **Closing-snapshot field-level null rules, exactly.** `originalGravity`, `finalGravity`, `abv`, `apparentAttenuationPct`, `beerVolumeL`, `beerVolumeSource` and `frozenAt` are **always non-null** (guaranteed by the completion gate). `mashEfficiencyPct` is `null` **iff** `measuredPreBoilGravity === null` **or** `recipeSnapshot.equipment.mashEfficiencyPct <= 0`. `peakFermentationTempC` is `null` **iff** no reading carries a non-`null` `tempC`. `primingSugarG` and `primingSugarEquivGPerL` are **both** non-`null` **iff** `carbonationType ∈ {'Sugar','KegSugar'}` **and** `carbonationVolumesTarget !== null` **and** `peakFermentationTempC !== null` **and** `beerVolumeL > 0`; otherwise **both** are `null` — they are the same quantity in two units and may never disagree about presence. `carbonationForcePsi` is non-`null` **iff** `carbonationType ∈ {'KegForce','KegForceQuick'}` **and** `carbonationVolumesTarget !== null` **and** `carbonationTempC !== null`; otherwise `null`.
- **Beer-volume precedence.** `measuredBottlingSizeL !== null` → `{ beerVolumeL: measuredBottlingSizeL, beerVolumeSource: 'measured' }`; otherwise `{ beerVolumeL: recipeSnapshot.equipment.batchSizeL, beerVolumeSource: 'recipe' }`. `!== null`, never falsy — a stored `0` is a present value, exactly as `resolveOriginalGravity` treats `0`. The `beerVolumeL > 0` guard in the priming-sugar rule above is what stops `0` producing `0/0 = NaN` in the g/L figure.
- **`bottlingDate` is server-owned and write-once, mirroring `fermentationStartDate` exactly.** Set by the server, to the transition's own `now`, **iff** the request transitions a batch whose stored `bottlingDate` is `null` into status `'Conditioning'`. Never overwritten (a `Conditioning → Conditioning` re-save leaves it byte-identical), never set by any other transition — in particular **not** by `Conditioning → Completed` — and never accepted from a client.
- **Three server-owned keys, one rejection mechanism.** A `PUT /api/batches/:id` body containing **any** of `fermentationStartDate`, `bottlingDate` or `closingSnapshot` — with any value, including the batch's own current one and `null` — is `400 VALIDATION_FAILED` naming the offending key. This comes from the existing `preValidation` hook, generalised to a named key list; **not** from `additionalProperties: false`, which Fastify's repo-wide `removeAdditional: true` default makes inert (M3_P1 critic Finding 1, M5_P1 AC-27). A `PUT /api/batches/:batchId/notes/:noteId` body containing `timestamp` or `status` is `400` by the same mechanism.
- **`null` in a string-typed field is rejected, not coerced.** Fastify's ajv defaults include `coerceTypes: true`, under which `null` against `{ type: 'string' }` coerces to `''`. `tasteNotes: null` and `note: null` are therefore rejected by the same `preValidation` raw-body inspection that already rejects `comment: null` (`routes/batches.ts:39-41`), generalised to a named key list. `''` remains the deliberate unset value for `tasteNotes` and is always admissible.
- **`note` is the one string field where `''` is rejected.** `BatchNoteWriteInput.note` has `minLength: 1`, `maxLength: 5000`; `''` is `400`. This is deliberately **unlike** `comment` and `tasteNotes`, where `''` is the unset value of an optional field on a larger object: a note is created by an explicit user action, and an empty note is a row that records nothing. Stated because the inconsistency is intentional and would otherwise read as an oversight.
- **Numeric bounds, exact operators.** All inclusive unless stated. `measuredFg`: `null` or `[0.900, 1.200]` — `0.899` and `1.201` are `400`, `0.900` and `1.200` are accepted; identical to `measuredOg` and a reading's `sg`. `measuredBottlingSizeL`: `null` or `[0.1, 1000]` — `0.09`, `0` and `1000.01` are `400`; the lower bound is positive because the value is a divisor. `carbonationVolumesTarget`: `null` or `[0, 5]` — `-0.01` and `5.01` are `400`, `0` and `5` are accepted (`0` = still beer, a real setting). `carbonationTempC`: `null` or `[-20, 50]` — the same band `tempC` and `grainTemperatureC` already use; `-20.01` and `50.01` are `400`. `tasteRating`: `null` or an **integer** in `[1, 5]` — `0`, `6` and **`4.5`** are all `400` (`type: 'integer'`, not `number`); `1` and `5` are accepted. `tasteNotes`: `string`, `maxLength: 5000`, `''` admissible, `null` `400`. `carbonationType`: `null` or a member of `CARBONATION_TYPES`; any other string is `400`.
- **Carbonation coefficients live in `carbonation.ts`, not `constants.ts`.** `packages/calculations/test/units.test.ts` asserts a **closed list of exactly 15 exported constant names** in `constants.ts` and is itself Untouched. The §3.7 polynomial coefficients and the sucrose factor are used by exactly one module and are not kit settings, so they are **module-local named constants inside `carbonation.ts`** (not exported), leaving `constants.ts` and `units.test.ts` byte-unchanged. This departs from M5_P1 §5's *"named in `constants.ts` only if they are published formula coefficients"* phrasing — §4 deviation 5.
- **Priming sugar is sucrose, and no conversion factor is invented.** M5_P1 §5 flagged that §3.7's `4 g/L/volume` factor is sucrose while build-spec §2's field is named `primingSugarEquiv(g/L)`. **Resolution: the figure is sucrose, and it is named and labelled as sucrose** — `primingSugarG` / `primingSugarEquivGPerL` are documented and displayed as *grams of sucrose (table sugar) equivalent*. No factor table for dextrose, DME or honey is introduced, because §3.7 supplies no source for one and this project does not ship unsourced brewing arithmetic. `carbonationType` distinguishes sugar-conditioning from keg-force, **not** sugar species.
- **`KegForceQuick` uses the same PSI figure as `KegForce`; no quick-carb formula is invented.** Build-spec §2 lists four `carbonationType` values but §3.7 supplies exactly one force-carbonation equation. `KegForceQuick` therefore reports the **same** equilibrium `forceCarbonationPsi` for the target volumes at the given temperature. Stated explicitly so no reader assumes a separate formula exists and none is fabricated.
- **`forceCarbonationPsi` is unclamped and may be negative.** The §3.7 polynomial is a fit with no stated valid domain and returns negative PSI at low volumes/temperatures (e.g. `{ volumesCO2: 0, tempC: 20 }` → ≈ `-11.9996`). M5_P1 §5 flagged this as a P2 decision. **Resolution: the pure function returns the raw polynomial value, unclamped**, on exactly the reasoning that keeps apparent attenuation unclamped (M5_P1 Resolved Ambiguities) — a clamp at `0` would hide an out-of-domain input behind a plausible number. The *input* domain is bounded instead, at the storage boundary: `carbonationVolumesTarget ∈ [0,5]` and `carbonationTempC ∈ [-20,50]` are enforced by JSON Schema, and the **UI suppresses the PSI display when the computed value is `< 0`**, showing an explicit out-of-range note rather than a negative pressure. Operator: `< 0` suppresses; exactly `0` displays as `0.0 PSI`.
- **`primingSugarG` is clamped at `0`, per the source.** Build-spec §3.7 wraps the result in `Math.max(0, …)` and that is transcribed, not second-guessed: a target below the beer's residual CO₂ needs **no** sugar, and `0` is the physically correct answer rather than a hidden negative. This is not inconsistent with the PSI rule above — there, negative output means the *input* was out of domain; here, negative intermediate means the *answer* is genuinely zero.
- **Peak fermentation temperature is derived from the reading log, never defaulted.** `peakFermentationTempC(readings)` is the maximum non-`null` `tempC` across all readings, or `null` when the array is empty or every `tempC` is `null` — **never `-Infinity`**, which is what an unguarded `Math.max(...[])` returns. With no temperature reading, the priming-sugar figure is **suppressed entirely** with an explicit on-screen prompt to log one. No default temperature is fabricated: a fabricated peak temperature silently changes the residual-CO₂ term and therefore the sugar dose, and an over-dose is a bottle-bomb hazard.
- **Measured mash efficiency is computed by one shared function, not two.** The derivation currently inlined in `apps/web/src/pages/BatchDetail.tsx:97-114` moves verbatim into `measuredMashEfficiencyPct` in `packages/calculations/src/batchClosing.ts`, and both the browser (live, for a batch that is not yet Completed) and the server (once, at the freeze) call it. `BatchDetail.tsx` stops importing `calculateVolumes`, `totalExtractPoints` and `gravityAtVolume` altogether. This is the duplication half of the M4_P1 carryover's resolution and is enforced by **AC-46**.
- **A Completed batch reads its comparison figures from `closingSnapshot`; the `null` branch is unreachable but specified.** No batch row in any database can currently be `Completed` — `BATCH_STATUSES` has three members and migration `0008` has not run — so `status === 'Completed' && closingSnapshot === null` is unreachable by construction. It is nonetheless a representable state, so the contract is stated rather than left to crash or to recompute silently: that branch **recomputes and labels the figures `(recalculated)`**, the same discipline M4_P1's AC-12(e) applies to a pre-0006 `statsSnapshot`. It is not required to be reachable in a test through HTTP; it is required to be provable at the component level with a hand-built prop.
- **One chronological ordering implementation for the whole repo.** Notes are ordered by `timestamp` **ascending**, ties broken by `id` **lexicographic ascending** — the identical rule readings already use. Rather than write a second comparator, a single `sortByInstantThenId` is introduced in a new `packages/calculations/src/chronology.ts`, and **both** `sortReadings` (existing) and `sortBatchNotes` (new) delegate to it. `sortReadings`'s observable contract — including the unparseable-instant-sorts-last rule — is unchanged, and M5_P1's `packages/calculations/test/fermentation.test.ts` stays **Untouched** and green as the regression proof.
- **`BatchWithReadings` gains `notes` and keeps its name.** The `GET /api/batches/:id` payload becomes `Batch & { readings: Reading[]; notes: BatchNote[] }` under the existing name. Renaming it to something like `BatchDetailPayload` would touch six files for cosmetics inside the MVP-closing phase, and introducing a second detail type would give one payload two names. - **`GET /api/batches` stays lean.** The list returns `Batch[]` with **neither** a `readings` key **nor** a `notes` key — the same reasoning that kept `readings` off it in M5_P1 and keeps `RecipeSummary` lean.
- **Migration `0008` is additive only** — one `CREATE TABLE batch_notes`, two `CREATE INDEX`, and nine `ALTER TABLE batches ADD COLUMN`. **No table rebuild of `batches`.** SQLite permits `ADD COLUMN … NOT NULL DEFAULT ''` (a `NOT NULL` added column requires a non-`NULL` default, which `taste_notes` has), so no rebuild should be necessary. **AC-16** exists to catch it if `drizzle-kit` emits one anyway — a rebuild silently drops the `recipe_id → recipes.id ON DELETE RESTRICT` edge while every test still passes. *If AC-16 fails, the documented fallback is to make `taste_notes` nullable and enforce the `''`-never-`null` rule at the type and route layers only* — that is a `/diagnose` route, not a licence to hand-edit generated SQL without re-checking the pragmas.
- **No database is wiped, and no already-applied migration is edited.** Migrations `0000`–`0007` and their snapshots are byte-unchanged; `meta/_journal.json` gains **exactly one** appended `idx: 8` entry. The existing dev database — including the one legacy pre-0006 batch row with `stats_snapshot NULL` — is migrated in place and every pre-existing row survives.
- **No new runtime dependency.** `package-lock.json` and every `package.json` are byte-unchanged. The carbonation math is arithmetic; the notes UI, the comparison table and the carbonation panel are plain React over existing primitives.
- **Scope guardrail is a SHA-256 content manifest, not `git diff`.** Independently re-confirmed this session: `git rev-list --count HEAD` is **1** and the sole commit `7d88e64` predates M1–M5 entirely, so a whole-tree diff cannot attribute any path to this phase. **AC-56** specifies the manifest method, including the two ISO-8601 timestamps that M5_P1's audit found were reconstructed from mtimes rather than recorded as the criterion required.

### 1.4 HTTP contract

| Method & path | Success | Failure |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/batches` | `200 Batch[]` — the nine new batch fields present; **no `readings` key, no `notes` key** | `500 INTERNAL` |
| `GET /api/batches/:id` | `200 BatchWithReadings` — `readings` and `notes` both in canonical order | `404 NOT_FOUND` |
| `POST /api/batches` | `201 BatchWithReadings` with `readings: []`, `notes: []`, all nine new fields at their creation defaults (`tasteNotes: ''`, everything else `null`) | `400 VALIDATION_FAILED`, `404 NOT_FOUND` |
| `PUT /api/batches/:id` | `200 BatchWithReadings` | `400 VALIDATION_FAILED` (bad body, disallowed transition, unmet completion gate, or a server-owned key present), `404 NOT_FOUND` |
| `POST /api/batches/:batchId/notes` | `201 BatchNote` — `timestamp` and `status` server-minted | `400 VALIDATION_FAILED`, `404 NOT_FOUND` (unknown batch) |
| `PUT /api/batches/:batchId/notes/:noteId` | `200 BatchNote` — replaces `note` **only**; `timestamp` and `status` byte-identical | `400 VALIDATION_FAILED`, `404 NOT_FOUND` |
| `DELETE /api/batches/:batchId/notes/:noteId` | `204`, empty body | `404 NOT_FOUND` |

Every non-2xx body is exactly `ApiErrorBody`, through the existing `sendApiError`. A **partial** `PUT` body on either resource is `400`, never a merge. There is no `PATCH`. **Note ids are scoped to their batch:** `PUT`/`DELETE` on a `noteId` that exists but belongs to a *different* `batchId` is `404 NOT_FOUND`, never a cross-batch write.

The reading routes (`POST`/`PUT`/`DELETE /api/batches/:batchId/readings*`) are **behaviourally unchanged** by this phase. `/api/recipes*`, `/api/equipment-profiles*`, `/api/mash-profiles*`, `/api/fermentation-profiles*`, `/api/catalog` and `/api/health` are unchanged.

### 2.6 Stateful integration contract — `apps/web/src/pages/BatchDetail.tsx`

- **Hook discipline.** Every hook — including the new note state, the new note-error state, and the memo producing the resolved comparison figures and carbonation figures — is declared **above** both existing early returns (`if (error)`, `if (!batch)`). This is M4_P1 finding F-2's regression guard, re-asserted by **AC-42**.
- **Error-state isolation, four states not three.** `error` gates the full-page load-failure early return. `saveError` gates the batch-save banner. `readingError` gates reading-mutation failures. A **fourth**, separate `noteError` gates note-mutation failures. A failed note write leaves the note list, the reading log, the chart, the comparison table and the entered note text **all on screen**.
- **The figure-resolution memo is the single branch point.** One memo resolves, for the whole page: `statsAreRecomputed` (existing), the estimated figures (existing `statsSnapshot` path), and the measured figures — reading `batch.closingSnapshot` when it is non-`null`, and otherwise computing live via `measuredMashEfficiencyPct`, `abvBalling`, `apparentAttenuationPct` and the carbonation functions. **When `closingSnapshot` is non-`null`, none of those derivation functions is called for that render.** `<MeasuredComparison>` and `<CarbonationPanel>` are pure presentational components over the memo's output and perform no arithmetic of their own beyond formatting and the measured−estimated delta.
- **Lockstep.** In the render immediately following a successful note create, edit or delete, the note list and any note-derived count reflect the new set, derived from the same `notes` array reference. No second `getBatch` fetch is required for them to agree. The same property already holds for readings (M5_P1 §2.6) and is unchanged.
- **The status `<select>` offers exactly `allowedNextStatuses(batch.status)`** — never a hand-written array. For `Completed` that array has length 1 and the select still renders, showing only `Completed`.
- **The seven new fields are edit-form fields**, submitted in the same `PUT` body, with the same `!== null` display discipline the existing measured fields use. `tasteRating` is rendered as a 1–5 control that can be cleared back to `null`.
- **Frozen figures stay frozen.** The `statsSnapshot` read path, the `(recalculated)` label, the attenuation panel, the chart and the reading log are behaviourally unchanged, and **no new `calculateRecipeStats` call site is introduced anywhere.**

### 2.7 Stateful integration contract — `apps/api/src/routes/batches.ts`

## 4. Deviation Register — requires explicit sign-off at the halt gate

Eleven points where this spec departs from `ROADMAP.md`'s literal wording, from `.gsd/documents/brewfather_clone_build_spec.md`, from an established convention in this repo, or from a commitment recorded in the archived M5_P1 spec. Each is reversible at this gate. **Entries 1–10 are unchanged and were already signed off; entry 11 was added by the 2026-08-09 third amendment and is the only item needing fresh sign-off.**

1. **The measured comparison figures are frozen in ONE `closingSnapshot` JSON column, not as individual measured columns.** Build-spec §2 lists `measuredAttenuation`, `measuredEfficiency` and `measuredAbv` as flat `Batch` fields. This spec stores the derived figures inside a single nullable JSON column instead, keeping only genuine **inputs** (`measuredFg`, `measuredBottlingSizeL`, the three carbonation settings) as flat client-writable columns. *Reasoning:* it mirrors `stats_snapshot` exactly — one column, one write rule, one structurally-unwritable-elsewhere guarantee — so the freeze discipline this project already audits transfers without a second pattern; it puts the derived figures where they cannot be reached by `BatchWriteInput` at all, rather than relying on schema discipline to keep three writable columns read-only; and it lets the snapshot carry its own provenance (`frozenAt`, `beerVolumeSource`, `peakFermentationTempC`) so a frozen figure is auditable without re-deriving anything. *Cost:* the figures are not directly SQL-queryable, which matters to nothing in the MVP. *Overrule and use flat columns:* `measured_abv`, `measured_attenuation`, `measured_efficiency`, `priming_sugar_equiv_g_per_l`, `carbonation_force_psi`, `peak_fermentation_temp_c`, `bottling_size_source` and `frozen_at` become eight nullable columns on `batches`, `ClosingSnapshot` disappears, AC-13/14/15 are rewritten per column, and the "structurally unwritable" guarantee becomes an eight-column discipline in `update()`'s `.set()` rather than a single omitted parameter.

2. **Completing a batch requires both `measuredOg` and `measuredFg`; it is a gate, not a nullable arm.** Nothing in the roadmap or build spec says a batch cannot be closed without a final gravity. *Reasoning:* the roadmap's own Milestone 5 threshold is *"calculated attenuation and ABV from the measured OG/FG match a hand-calculation"* — both are inputs to the thing the milestone is verified by; and requiring them makes `ClosingSnapshot.abv` / `.apparentAttenuationPct` **total**, removing a nullable arm that would otherwise propagate into every consumer and every criterion. *Cost:* a brewer who genuinely never took a final gravity cannot mark the batch Completed. *Overrule and allow completion without them:* `abv` and `apparentAttenuationPct` become `number | null` in `ClosingSnapshot`, AC-21 is rewritten as a permissive case, and AC-37/AC-40 gain suppression rows for a Completed-but-unmeasured batch — roughly 3 additional criteria and one more nullable arm through the whole display path.

3. **`bottlingDate` is server-owned and write-once at the first `Conditioning`, not a client-entered date.** Build-spec §2 lists it as a plain `Batch` field. *Reasoning:* it mirrors `fermentationStartDate` exactly — a proven, already-audited mechanism (M5_P1 AC-30) — so the two lifecycle dates behave identically instead of one being trustworthy and one being whatever a client last sent; and a client-settable packaging date has no defence against drifting later than the readings it must bound. *Cost:* a brewer who logs the transition a day late gets a `bottlingDate` a day late, with no in-app correction path. *Overrule and make it client-writable:* it joins `BatchWriteInput` with an ISO-8601 pattern and a `Date.parse` reality check (the M5_P1 AC-24 pattern), leaves `SERVER_OWNED_BATCH_KEYS`, and AC-22 is rewritten as a round-trip criterion.

4. **The M4_P1 measured-efficiency carryover is resolved HERE, and resolving it means editing the block M5_P1's AC-32 protected.** M5_P1's AC-32 required `BatchDetail.tsx`'s measured-efficiency `useMemo` block to be behaviourally unchanged, precisely so the deferral could not widen. This phase **deliberately deletes it**, extracting it verbatim into `measuredMashEfficiencyPct` and freezing its output at `Completed`. *This is the resolution AC-32 was holding the door open for, not a violation of it* — stated explicitly so the critic does not read it as one. *Reasoning:* the fix M5_P1 named ("freeze the measured comparison figures when the batch is closed") is being built here anyway, and the duplication half is free once the function exists in `packages/calculations`. **AC-10** pins the extraction as behaviour-preserving (strict `===` against the transcribed original composition) and **AC-38** pins the freeze against a formula swap. *Overrule and defer again:* not offered — this is the third phase the observation has been open and M5_P1 committed this phase to it by name.

5. **The §3.7 coefficients are module-local inside `carbonation.ts`, not exported from `constants.ts`.** M5_P1 §5's preview said coefficients should be *"named in `constants.ts` only if they are published formula coefficients rather than kit settings"* — and these are published coefficients. *Reasoning for departing:* `packages/calculations/test/units.test.ts` asserts a **closed list of exactly 15 exported constant names** and is on this spec's Untouched list; adding nine coefficients would mean editing that test's closed list and count, i.e. a named exception on an Untouched test file, to gain nothing — these values are used by exactly one module, are never composed with other calculations, and are not kit settings a user can change. *Overrule and export them:* `constants.ts` and `units.test.ts` both move to Modified with the closed list going 15 → 24, and AC-47's single-occurrence grep is retargeted at `constants.ts`.

6. **`KegForceQuick` reports the same PSI as `KegForce`, and no sugar-species conversion factor is introduced.** Build-spec §2 names four carbonation types and a field called `primingSugarEquiv`; §3.7 supplies exactly one force-carbonation equation and one sucrose factor. *Reasoning:* this project does not ship unsourced brewing arithmetic, and an invented quick-carb pressure or dextrose factor would be exactly that — with a physical-safety consequence in the sugar case. The honest resolution is to compute what the source supports and **label it** (grams of sucrose; equilibrium PSI). *Cost:* a brewer priming with dextrose must convert externally, and `KegForceQuick` is a labelling distinction rather than a different number. *Overrule:* supply a sourced factor table (dextrose ≈ 1.1×, DME ≈ 1.5× sucrose by mass are the commonly cited figures, but **a source must be named in the spec**, not assumed) and/or a sourced quick-carb pressure rule — roughly 3 additional criteria and one new constant family.

7. **`BatchWithReadings` gains `notes` and keeps its now-imprecise name.** *Reasoning:* renaming it to `BatchDetailPayload` would touch `packages/shared-types/src/batches.ts`, `apps/api/src/repositories/batchRepository.ts`, `apps/api/src/routes/batches.ts`, `apps/web/src/api/client.ts`, `apps/web/src/pages/BatchDetail.tsx` and `apps/web/test/helpers/fixtures.ts` for a cosmetic gain, inside the phase that must end with a clean MVP acceptance run; and introducing a second detail type would give one payload two names. The doc comment is corrected instead. *Overrule and rename:* those six files move onto the Modified list with the rename as their only permitted edit, plus the two test files that reference the type.

8. **Note editing is allowed (text only); notes are not append-only.** A defensible alternative is that a timestamped log entry should never be rewritten. *Reasoning for allowing it:* `timestamp` and `status` are structurally unwritable (omitted from `.set()`, rejected by `preValidation`), so an edit can only change the prose — it cannot falsify **when** or **at what stage** the note was made; and the alternative correction path, delete-and-repost, destroys the original timestamp, which is strictly worse for a log. A brew log whose typos are permanent gets abandoned. *Overrule and make notes append-only:* drop `PUT /api/batches/:batchId/notes/:noteId`, `updateNote`, `updateBatchNote` and the edit affordance; AC-32 keeps only its delete and cross-batch halves.

9. **The `DryHop` `timeMinutes` retirement is DEFERRED OUT OF MILESTONE 5 — a decision with a stated placement, not a third silent inheritance.** M4_P1's approved spec assigned it to "M5"; M5_P1's deviation 9 placed it in P2. It is not built here. **Fresh reasoning, not M5_P1's:** *(a)* This phase's defining event is the **MVP acceptance run**, whose threshold is that the entire history reads back intact. The retirement is a `recipe_hops` migration plus a `HopItem` model change — a change to the very recipe surface the acceptance run's frozen snapshot is taken from. If it lands in the same execute pass, a failed acceptance run cannot be attributed between *"the batch pipeline is wrong"* and *"the recipe model moved underneath it"*, which is precisely the diagnosability argument the roadmap used to keep the monorepo refactor out of M2. *(b)* The carryover is **stable, not rotting**: M4_P1's AC-10 already proved `time_minutes` nullable, DryHop-only, round-tripping verbatim through `PUT /api/recipes/:id` at `4320`, and `calculateRecipeStats` bit-identical at `0` vs `4320`. There is no accruing correctness debt — only a naming/model debt. *(c)* Its real value is a **dry-hop schedule** (day offset and duration measured from `fermentationStartDate`) surfaced in the recipe designer and on the batch timeline — a user-visible vertical slice of its own, not a rider on a closing phase. M5_P1 supplied the referent, so it is now **ready to be scheduled**, which is exactly why it deserves a slot rather than another inheritance. *(d)* It is required by **nothing** in the MVP bar (`.gsd/DISCOVERY.md`), Milestone 5's roadmap threshold, or build-spec §1.2's Fermenting/Conditioning bullets. **Definition of done, stated so it stops being vague for whoever picks it up:** drop `recipe_hops.time_minutes` in a new migration; `HopItem` gains `dryHopDayOffset: number | null` and `dryHopDurationDays: number | null`; `classifyHopUse` and `calculateSingleHopIbu` are reviewed and proved unchanged for all five uses; M1's six fixture `CalculatedStats` are re-verified bit-identical; `HopSection.tsx` surfaces the two fields for `use === 'DryHop'` only; the batch's fermentation timeline marks the dry-hop window. **This needs a placement decision at the halt gate** — it is item (c) there. *Overrule and it ships in this phase:* add the model change, the migration, the `HopSection.tsx` surface and an M1 fixture re-verification — roughly 10 additional criteria, a reopened M1 tolerance argument, and a recipe-schema migration in the same pass as the MVP acceptance run.

10. **`apps/web/test/App.test.tsx` is carved off the Untouched list into a named §1.5 exception — added 2026-08-09, closing the `/diagnose` finding raised against the otherwise-complete build.** *What was wrong:* this spec contradicted itself, in the same way M5_P1's deviation 10 did and for the same underlying reason. §1.3 makes `BatchWithReadings.notes` a **required** field, while §1.5 simultaneously named `apps/web/test/App.test.tsx` as byte-unchanged — and that file's line 117 builds a `BatchWithReadings` as an object literal (`{ ...createdBatch, readings: [] }`), which a new required key mechanically stops compiling.
